# Fraction to MM Calculator

Convert fractional inch measurements to millimeters quickly and easily.

## Common Conversions

- 1/4" = 6.35 mm
- 3/8" = 9.525 mm
- 7/16" = 11.1125 mm
- 5/8" = 15.875 mm
- 1/64" = 0.396875 mm

The standard conversion is based on:

**1 inch = 25.4 mm**
